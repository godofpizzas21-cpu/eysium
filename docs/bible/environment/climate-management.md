## 1. The Corridor

Atmospheric CO₂ stands at **340 ppm**, in the middle of the constitutional
corridor of **320–360 ppm** (`gov.constitution` §2.3). It has been inside the
corridor since EY 210, after 122 years of managed decline from a Concord-era peak
of 505 ppm — itself down from the Long Emergency maximum of 610 ppm
(`hist.timeline` §8).

Two things about the corridor are worth stating precisely, because Elysians
themselves make the distinction constantly.

**It has a floor, not only a ceiling.** Below 320 ppm the Concord is
constitutionally required to *stop drawing down*. The floor exists because
photosynthesis, agricultural yield, and the biosphere the Concord actually has
are all adapted to the current concentration, and because the founders judged
that a civilization capable of engineering its atmosphere downward would
eventually be tempted to keep going. The floor is the restraint virtue written
as a number.

**It is a corridor, not a target.** The Concord does not aim for a particular
figure inside the band and does not treat drift within it as failure. What
triggers action is approach to either edge, and the trigger is automatic.

## 2. The Carbon Account

The **Ecological Commission** publishes a planetary carbon account quarterly. It
measures, publishes, and on a threshold breach compels a response; it cannot make
policy (`gov.institutions` §6).

| Flow | Annual | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross residual emissions** | 1.9 Gt CO₂ | Cement, agriculture, industrial process chemistry, land-use change |
| **Removal** | 2.1 Gt CO₂ | See below |
| **Net position** | −0.2 Gt CO₂ | Slight net drawdown, holding position against outgassing |

Removal is split across three methods, deliberately rather than by cost:

| Method | Annual | Energy cost |
|---|---|---|
| Biological — restoration, soil carbon, blue carbon | 0.9 Gt | Negligible |
| Direct air capture | 0.8 Gt | **0.12 TW — 0.27% of planetary energy** |
| Enhanced weathering | 0.4 Gt | Included in materials handling |

Direct air capture is astonishingly cheap in energy terms on a fusion-powered
planet: a quarter of one percent of generation removes 0.8 Gt a year. This is the
single clearest illustration of why cheap abundant energy is treated in Elysian
policy as an *environmental* technology rather than an industrial one.

The diversification is not accidental. Biological removal is cheap and
reversible — a forest can burn. Direct air capture is expensive and permanent.
Enhanced weathering is slow and durable. The Commission maintains all three
because a removal portfolio with one method is a removal portfolio with one
failure mode.

### The breach procedure

If the account projects the corridor edge being crossed within 10 years, the
Commission issues a **threshold notice**. A notice compels the Executive Board to
publish a response within 90 days and the Assembly to debate it. If the edge is
actually crossed, a planetary environmental emergency is triggered automatically
under Article 12 — no declaration required, no discretion available.

Three threshold notices have been issued: EY 288 (approach to the ceiling after a
decade of high cement output), EY 341, and EY 396 (both approaches to the
**floor**, caused by removal capacity overshooting during periods of low
industrial demand). No edge has been crossed since EY 210.

That two of three notices concerned the floor is treated in Concord commentary as
mildly embarrassing and quietly reassuring.

## 3. The Overturning Watch

The ocean overturning circulation is the Concord's most consequential slow
variable (`planet.climate` §3). A sustained 20% weakening triggers a
constitutional emergency automatically.

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Current strength | 96% of pre-industrial |
| Trough during the Long Emergency | 79% |
| Emergency trigger | 80% |
| Monitoring array | 1,410 fixed stations, continuous |
| Detection confidence for a 20% shift | 94% within 4 years |

The honest problem is in that last row and canon states it: **the Concord cannot
detect the trigger condition quickly.** Overturning strength is noisy on annual
timescales, and distinguishing a real 20% weakening from natural variability
takes about four years of data. If the circulation began weakening tomorrow, the
constitutional emergency would fire around EY 416, by which point the cause would
be four years old.

The Commission has proposed a precautionary trigger at 88% three times. The
Council of Regions has rejected it three times, on the grounds that an automatic
planetary emergency with a 94%-confidence threshold is a defensible constitutional
device and one with a 60%-confidence threshold is not. Canon records this as a
genuine and unresolved disagreement about how to write a rule for a system that
gives ambiguous warnings.

## 4. Sea Level and the Coasts

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Sea level, relative to pre-industrial | **+0.62 m** |
| Committed further rise, recognised 38 BE | +0.9 m total, of which 0.28 m remains |
| Total cryospheric budget | 4.6 m (`planet.climate` §6) |
| Coastal population behind engineered defence | 410 million |
| Coastal population in managed-retreat programmes | 31 million |

The 4.6 m total budget is the Concord's structural good fortune, and Elysian
coastal planning says so openly: Elysium is **not one ice sheet away from
catastrophe**, because it does not have an ice sheet of that scale
(`planet.climate` §6). Defences are designed to a defensible worst case rather
than an open-ended one, which is a luxury Earth planners would recognise as
enormous.

**Managed retreat is a normal public programme, not a crisis measure.** 31
million Elysians live in areas scheduled for withdrawal over the coming century,
with compensation at full replacement value, relocation of whole Communes
together where residents choose it, and — the provision Elysians consider
essential — the right to keep the Commune as a legal and social entity even after
its physical site is abandoned. Communes that have relocated retain their name,
their register, their participatory allocation, and their seat in District
structures.

Where retreat is not chosen, defence is hard and expensive. Where neither works,
canon does not pretend otherwise: eleven small Isle settlements have been lost
outright since EY 300, and the Naming (`cult.foundations` §3) in the Sable Group
includes the names of places as well as people.

## 5. Living With the Hazards

The hazard table canonized in `planet.climate` §8 is the design basis for
everything below. Elysian adaptation follows one doctrine, inherited from the
tectonic and disaster answers elsewhere in canon: **early detection, absorbed
impact, rapid restoration.**

| Hazard | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Tropical cyclone (Serrance, Elandric coast) | 285 km/h structural design basis; mangrove-analogue and reef buffers; evacuation infrastructure sized for 90% of exposed population in 36 civil hours |
| Austral winter storm | Long-duration design; the grid's 14-day islanding rule (`energy.grid` §1) is largely an austral requirement |
| Monsoon failure | Food reserves sized against three consecutive warm Amarant phases (Phase 11) |
| Sirocc drought | Fossil-aquifer drawdown ceilings; desalination and transfer capacity held idle against multi-year events |
| Alcyon flood | 1-in-500-year design basis; floodplain reconnection rather than channelisation |
| Heat extremes | Urban design standards, cool-refuge provision in every Commune, and a statutory work-cessation temperature |
| Reef bleaching | Thermal-refuge identification, assisted gene flow, and 1.4 °C local anomaly as the intervention trigger |

**Reef intervention is the contested one.** Assisted gene flow — moving
heat-tolerant Stonebloom lineages into vulnerable reefs — is permitted under
strict licence, and 2,400 km² has been treated. Its opponents argue the Concord
is engineering an ecosystem it does not understand in order to preserve an
appearance of it; its defenders argue that declining to act is also a choice with
consequences. The Ecological Commission publishes both positions in its annual
account and takes neither.

## 6. Known Weaknesses

| Weakness | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Slow overturning detection** | Four years to 94% confidence; the constitutional trigger cannot fire faster than the ocean speaks, and the precautionary alternative has been rejected three times |
| **Removal capacity overshoot** | Two of three threshold notices concerned the *floor*; removal infrastructure is easier to build than to modulate |
| **Committed rise is unavoidable** | 0.28 m of the recognised commitment remains and no policy affects it |
| **Settlements lost** | Eleven Isle settlements gone since EY 300; adaptation has a lower bound and canon names it |
| **Assisted gene flow is unresolved** | Permitted, contested, and expanding without an agreed answer to the objection against it |
| **Cement** | Residual process emissions from cement are the largest single component of the 1.9 Gt and have resisted forty years of substitution research |

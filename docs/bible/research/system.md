## 1. Scale and Funding

| Indicator | Value |

| Dedicated researchers | 44 million |

| Funding source | Share |
|---|---|
| Public — Concord Research Fund, Regional councils, university block grants | 61% |
| Stewardship foundations | 17% |
| Private firms | 22% |

The **Concord Research Fund** is the largest single funder and operates on the
same model as public risk capital (`econ.markets` §2): it publishes its entire
portfolio including failures, and its programme-level failure rate — currently
38% — is treated as evidence of appropriate risk rather than of waste. A Fund
whose projects mostly succeed is understood to be funding the wrong projects.

**Stewardship foundations** (`econ.markets` §1) matter disproportionately here.
Their purpose-bound structure, with no owner who can sell and no distributable
profit, makes them the natural home for research on horizons no firm and few
governments will hold: seed stock, antimicrobial development, materials
substitution, and the century programmes of §4.

## 2. Research Integrity

The Concord's research practices are unusual, and every one of them descends
from the Public Record Act's premise that a civilization which hides its failures
loses the ability to detect the next one (`hist.timeline` §13).

**Pre-registration is mandatory** for any publicly funded study with a
hypothesis. Method, analysis plan, and stopping rules are deposited before data
collection, and departures from the plan must be declared in the publication.

**The Negative Register.** Every funded project deposits its result, including
null results, failed methods, and abandoned lines, in a public archive. Deposit
is a condition of the grant, not a courtesy. The Register holds 41 million
entries and is consulted more often than the published literature by researchers
designing new work, because knowing what has already failed is worth more per
unit of reading than knowing what has succeeded.

**Replication is a funded line, not a favour.** 12% of the entire research
budget — đ6.7 trillion — funds replication of others' work, conducted by
laboratories with no stake in the outcome. Replication carries the same career
credit as original work.

**Adversarial review.** Alongside ordinary peer review, any major finding
attracts a **red grant**: funding awarded to a team whose explicit task is to
find the finding wrong. Red teams publish independently and are paid the same
whether they succeed or fail. This is the minority-report habit
(`gov.constitution` §2.4) applied to science, and Elysian researchers treat
surviving a red grant as the real credential.

**No paywalls.** All publicly funded research publishes openly, with data and
code, in perpetuity, held by the Record Office. Publication is treated as public
infrastructure rather than as a market.

**Does it work?** Partly. **71% of prominent findings replicate successfully** —
substantially better than the Integration achieved, and not the 100% the system's
architects hoped for. The Concord publishes the figure annually and its research
councils describe the remaining 29% as an unsolved problem rather than an
acceptable rate.

## 3. Restraint: What the Concord Does Not Research

The civic virtue of restraint (`cult.foundations` §1) applies to inquiry as much
as to power. A short list of research is prohibited or tightly licensed, and
canon is specific that this is a genuine cost.

| Area | Status | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| Enhancing pathogen transmissibility or lethality | **Prohibited absolutely** | The Kessander Plague (`hist.timeline` §8) |
| Autonomous weapons and lethal target selection | **Prohibited absolutely** | The Abolition and the Cassian Incident |
| Heritable germline modification in Elysians | Prohibited except to prevent a specific severe disorder, under judicial licence | Phase 9 |
| Non-consensual neural reading or writing | **Prohibited absolutely** | Charter right 7 (privacy), non-derogable |
| AI capability research above defined thresholds | Licensed, audited, with mandatory disclosure | Phase 13 |
| Deep-sea ecosystem disturbance for research | Licensed only where non-extractive | Article 12 |

**The prohibition on pathogen enhancement is the one Elysians argue about.**
Its critics — including a standing minority within the Concord's own biosecurity
establishment — hold that the Concord cannot prepare for engineered threats it
refuses to study, and that the prohibition trades a known present safety for an
unknown future vulnerability. Its defenders point out that the Kessander Plague
was built by people who had read the literature. Canon records the argument as
live and the prohibition as holding.

## 4. Century Programmes

The zero pure time preference (`econ.money` §5) makes something possible that
few Earth research systems achieved: research programmes with commitments longer
than any career, protected from annual budget cycles by statute and reviewed
through the intergenerational account.

There are **31 century programmes**. The oldest, and the ones Elysians name first:

- **The Soil Series** (from EY 61) — continuous measurement of soil carbon and
  structure at 14,000 fixed plots. It is why canon can state that Elysian soil is
  accumulating (`planet.resources` §6).
- **The Veydran Cores** (from EY 88) — deep ice and sediment records underpinning
  the paleoclimate canon of `planet.climate` §7.
- **Substitution** (from EY 140) — materials replacement for the Constrained
  List. Two hundred years old, and its principal target remains unmet (§6).
- **The Restoration Trials** (from EY 154) — 900 paired sites testing restoration
  methods against controls, on an 80–140 year horizon to functional equivalence
  (`env.conservation` §2).
- **The Overturning Array** (from EY 203) — 1,410 stations, the evidentiary basis
  for the constitutional trigger (`env.climate` §3).

A century programme may be ended only by the Assembly, on a published finding
that its question has been answered or is unanswerable, and after the Office of
Future Generations has been heard. Four have been ended; none for cost.

## 5. The Veydran Commons

Veydra was settled last and thinly (`hist.timeline` §2), which is why it was
available to become something no other continent could be.

The whole of Veydra — both Regions, 140 million residents, and the Ice Cap — is
governed for research purposes by a **compact of all 34 Regions**
(`gov.regions` §6), not by Veydra alone. Veydrans consented to this at the
founding and renew it every twenty years by regional referendum; the last renewal
carried with 71%.

It hosts what cold, dark, quiet, and stable make possible: the ice and sediment
cores, the deep-time seismic array, the principal optical and infrared
observatories, cryogenic physics, and the long-duration isolation facilities used
in medicine and neuroscience. Highmarch is the guardian jurisdiction for the Ice
Cap, which is inviolable under Article 12.

The arrangement is not frictionless. Veydrans receive substantial fiscal
equalization (`econ.money` §6) and a permanent seat in every research council,
and still describe the compact in their own politics as *"hosting the planet's
laboratory and being asked to be grateful for it."* Canon takes the complaint
seriously; it recurs at every renewal and has never been resolved, only renewed.

## 6. Known Weaknesses

| Weakness | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Replication at 71%** | Better than any historical benchmark and not what the system was built for; the residual is unexplained and the councils refuse to call it acceptable |
| **Substitution has failed** | Two centuries of a protected century programme have not replaced beryllium, and canon does not pretend the programme is close |
| **Prestige concentration** | Roughly forty institutions absorb a disproportionate share of funding and produce a disproportionate share of senior appointments; no mechanism has reduced this (`edu.lifelong` §6) |
| **Negative Register compliance** | Deposit is a grant condition; audited compliance is 84%, and the missing 16% is concentrated in private and foundation-funded work where enforcement is weakest |
| **Moratoria have costs** | The pathogen prohibition in particular is defended on grounds that cannot be tested, since the counterfactual is unobservable by construction |
| **Veydran consent is renewed, not settled** | A twenty-year referendum on hosting the planet's research is a standing constitutional risk that everyone has agreed not to look at directly |
| **Red grants attract a type** | Adversarial review works, and it has produced a small professional class of career critics whose incentives are not obviously better than the ones they check |

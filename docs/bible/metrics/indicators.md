# The Indicators

**Document ID:** `metric.indicators`
**Status:** Proposed
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Authoritative data:** `data/metrics.json`
**Inherits:** `metric.system` (the six principles, the Unmeasured Register), and
every domain chapter from which these figures derive

All figures as of **EY 412, Calenth 16**. Every value here is the same value
recorded in its domain dataset; where the two would differ, the domain dataset
governs and this chapter is wrong.

---

## How to Read This

Each indicator states its value, its unit, its direction of travel, and the
dataset it derives from. Two entries are not drawn from administrative data:
**life evaluation** and **financial distress** come from the planetary survey,
and **biodiversity integrity** is a declared composite. Both are flagged as such,
because principle 5 requires that a number's provenance be as visible as the
number.

Trends are recorded as improving, stable, worsening, or contested — the last
meaning the direction depends on which measure of the same thing is used, and the
Concord declines to resolve it.

## The Six Indicators the Concord Watches Most

Elysian public debate returns to the same handful, and canon should say which:

1. **Wealth Gini (0.44)** — flat for ninety years; the clearest unsolved
   distributional problem in the Concord.
2. **Overturning strength (96%)** — the slow variable with a constitutional
   trigger that cannot fire quickly.
3. **Beryllium horizon (120 years)** — the constraint every energy argument
   eventually reaches.
4. **Late-life cognitive decline (21% of over-110s)** — the largest cause of
   disability, with no prevention and no reversal.
5. **Reoffending (18%)** — the target unmet for forty years.
6. **Trust in the Concord tier** — measured performance exceeds public
   perception, and nobody has explained why (`gov.administration` §4).

None of them is improving quickly. Elysians regard a list of six stubborn
problems, published annually and argued about openly, as roughly what a healthy
civilization's indicator set should look like.

## Known Weaknesses

| Weakness | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Two soft indicators carry weight** | Life evaluation and financial distress are self-reported and are cited in policy as though they were administrative counts |
| **One declared composite** | Biodiversity integrity aggregates judgement calls into a single index, which principle 1 forbids at the headline level and permits within a domain — an inconsistency the Statistical Council acknowledges |
| **Contested trends resolve nothing** | Recording a trend as contested is honest and gives policy no direction |
| **Coverage is uneven** | Twelve domains, and culture, mobility, and off-world life are thinly covered because their measures are poor |

# Master Glossary

**Document ID:** `charter.glossary`
**Status:** Canon (entries marked ⏳ are Proposed)
**Version:** 1.23.0

Every in-world proper noun and project term is registered here before first
canonical use (`CANON_RULES.md` §3.1). Entries are alphabetical. Each entry:
**Term** — type — definition — *(source document ID)*.

---

## A

**Abolition, the** ⏳ — event — Verified dismantling of all strategic weapons, completed EY 34. *(hist.timeline)*

**Alcyon flow-share** ⏳ — institution — Rotating irrigation allocation first attested ~8,600 BE; oldest continuous institution on Elysium and ancestor of the 8-day week. *(hist.timeline)*

**Alcyon lungfish** ⏳ — species — Zoaea surviving multi-year drought encysted in mud; cultural symbol of resilience. *(planet.biosphere)*

**Alcyon Trunk** ⏳ — rail corridor — Maglev line following the River Alcyon from the delta to the Meridian Plain, linking Elysium's oldest urban corridor to the seat of the Assembly. *(route.transport)*

**Alcyon, River** ⏳ — river — Elysium's longest river (5,850 km), Cindral Arc to Solward Ocean across Meridia. *(planet.geography)*

**Alcyonic** ⏳ — language family — Largest Meridian family, descended from the flow-share administrative language. *(cult.languages)*

**Alcyonic ancestral practice** ⏳ — tradition — Continuous lineage-and-memory tradition of the Alcyon basin; 14% of the population. *(cult.foundations)*

**Amarant Ocean** ⏳ — ocean — Eastern ocean; monsoon engine of Elandris. *(planet.geography)*

**Amarant Oscillation** ⏳ — climate cycle — 4.1-year coupled ocean–atmosphere oscillation; delays Elandric monsoon onset in its warm phase. *(planet.climate)*

**Amarant Upwelling** ⏳ — current — Nutrient upwelling off western Elandris; fisheries cornerstone. *(planet.geography)*

**Amarant War** ⏳ — war — Elandris–Auroria conflict over fisheries and shipping lanes, 49–43 BE. *(hist.timeline)*

**Andrivar** ⏳ — city — Seat of the five Independent Offices, in Andriel, Elandris. *(city.urbanism)*

**Archaeoforms** ⏳ — clade — Chemosynthetic, nitrogen-fixing and extremophile microbial life. *(planet.biosphere)*

**Assembly, the** ⏳ — institution — Directly elected chamber of 720 members; the Concord's primary legislature. *(gov.institutions)*

**Atlas, the Elysium** — software — The browser-based 3D visualization application of Project Elysium. *(charter.project-charter)*

**Audit principle** ⏳ — constitutional principle — Every organ of government is audited by a body it cannot appoint, fund, or discipline. *(gov.constitution)*

**Audit Service** ⏳ — independent office — Audits every organ of government; rebuilt after the Corran Scandal. *(gov.institutions)*

**Aumar** ⏳ — month — Seventh month; southward equinox. *(hist.calendar)*

**Auroria** ⏳ — continent — Boreal northern continent; taiga, ores, long winters. *(planet.geography)*

**Aurorian Taiga** ⏳ — forest — Largest contiguous forest on Elysium. *(planet.geography)*

**Austral glider** ⏳ — species — Largest flying animal on Elysium; 6.4 m wingspan. *(planet.biosphere)*

**Austral Ocean** ⏳ — ocean — South polar ocean ringing Veydra's Ice Margin; stormiest waters. *(planet.geography)*

**Automation disclosure** ⏳ — labour rule — Firms above 250 workers must publish displacing automation two years ahead; surprise, not automation, is what makes displacement destructive. *(ind.industry)*

## B

**Baseline allowance** ⏳ — energy policy — Free household energy sized to ordinary needs; 61% of households never exceed it. Framed as delivery of the commons right, not as a subsidy. *(energy.grid)*

**BE** — notation — "Before the Era"; year count preceding EY 1. *(charter.canonical-units)*

**Beat** ⏳ — civil time unit — 1/60 of a civil minute; 0.99615 SI seconds. *(hist.calendar)*

**Boreal Ocean** ⏳ — ocean — North polar ocean, seasonally ice-covered above 78° N. *(planet.geography)*

**Boreal-Vail** ⏳ — language family — Agglutinative Aurorian family; the language of early industry. *(cult.languages)*

## C

**Calenth** ⏳ — month — Eighth month; contains the Bible's reference date. *(hist.calendar)*

**Candour** ⏳ — civic virtue — Say what is true, especially when it is against you; the opposing vice is presentation. *(cult.foundations)*

**Canon** — project term — An approved, binding fact. See `charter.canon-rules`. *(charter.canon-rules)*

**Cape Serrance** ⏳ — cape — Storm-wracked SE promontory of Elandris. *(planet.geography)*

**Carbon account** ⏳ — instrument — Quarterly planetary account of emissions and removal published by the Ecological Commission; net position currently −0.2 Gt. *(env.climate)*

**Cassel** ⏳ — sport — The planetary game: eight a side from the eight-day week, continuous play, played two-handed with a six-digit grip. *(cult.life)*

**Cassian Incident** ⏳ — event — 19 BE; an automated warning system escalated a sensor fault to launch-ready status, averted in 90 seconds by one officer's refusal. Origin of Concord AI governance. *(hist.timeline)*

**Cassian Rules** ⏳ — statute — Four binding rules on artificial systems: no automated escalation, legible uncertainty, the named human, and preserved refusal. *(ai.governance)*

**Cassian Station** ⏳ — installation — The northern Meridian warning site where a correctly functioning correlation system produced a false launch signature in 19 BE. *(ai.governance)*

**Cassine** ⏳ — instrument — Fretted twelve-string instrument played with all six digits of the stopping hand; Elysium's nearest thing to a universal instrument. *(cult.arts)*

**Century programme** ⏳ — research instrument — Research committed beyond any career, protected from annual budget cycles; 31 exist and four have ended, none for cost. *(res.system)*

**Cindral Arc** ⏳ — mountain range — Great eastern mountain system of Meridia; seismic, geothermal. *(planet.geography)*

**Cindral, Mount** ⏳ — peak — Highest mountain on Elysium (7,940 m), Cindral Arc. *(planet.geography)*

**Civic Income** ⏳ — transfer — Unconditional payment delivering Charter right 11; 34% of median income to every adult, never withdrawn as earnings rise. Does not replace healthcare, education, or housing. *(econ.markets)*

**Civilization Bible, the** — document — The complete reference work describing the Elysian Concord. *(charter.project-charter)*

**Commune** ⏳ — tier of government — Smallest tier, averaging 151,000 residents; holds the participatory allocation and the local objection. *(gov.regions)*

**Commune health post** ⏳ — facility — One per Commune; first contact, prevention, home-care coordination, and the seat of the named clinician. *(health.system)*

**Commune learning centre** ⏳ — institution — One per Commune; adult, part-time, and community learning, and where most retraining happens. *(edu.lifelong)*

**Commune Response Teams** ⏳ — service — Unarmed health and de-escalation professionals who resolve 61% of emergency calls without police involvement. *(law.policing)*

**Community Liaison** ⏳ — police branch — Officers attached to Communes with no enforcement role; the branch Elysians trust most (72%). *(law.policing)*

**Compact** ⏳ — instrument — Binding agreement between Regions requiring no planetary approval; 340 are active. *(gov.regions)*

**Comparative Governance Register** ⏳ — institution — Record Office register tracking outcomes across the four regional governing forms. *(gov.regions)*

**Concentration Board** ⏳ — body — Enforces the structural competition regime from within Treasury and Materials; appealable only to the Court of Review. *(ind.concentration)*

**Concord Charter, the** ⏳ — constitution — Constitution of the Elysian Concord, in force since EY 1; 41,000 words in 41 equally authoritative languages. *(gov.constitution)*

**Concord Criminal Floor** ⏳ — instrument — Minimum protections every regional criminal code must satisfy; a floor, never a ceiling. *(law.substantive)*

**Concord Investigation Office** ⏳ — body — Investigates planetary offences only; no patrol, no routine arrest powers, and must act through District services. *(law.policing)*

**Concord Monetary Authority** ⏳ — institution — Issues the dram under a three-limb mandate with no fixed priority, and must publish which limb it prioritised and why. *(econ.money)*

**Concord of Nine Cities** ⏳ — polity — Failed classical defensive league (840 BE) that gave the modern Concord its political vocabulary and name. *(hist.timeline)*

**Concord Research Fund** ⏳ — institution — Largest single research funder; publishes its whole portfolio including a 38% programme failure rate, because a Fund whose projects mostly succeed is funding the wrong projects. *(res.system)*

**Concord Service** ⏳ — institution — The Concord's combined defence and disaster organisation, 4.9 million strong and smaller than the police; 84% of it is the Response Corps. *(mil.service)*

**Concordial** ⏳ — language — Contact language descended from the Long Reach maritime pidgin; the Concord's working language, spoken as a second language by 71%. *(cult.languages)*

**Conflict registry** ⏳ — integrity system — Conflicts must be registered before acting; acting on an unregistered conflict is an offence in itself. *(gov.administration)*

**Connectivity corridor** ⏳ — protection tier — Working landscapes managed for passage; 71% of protected land sits in connected networks, because a reserve a species cannot leave is an enclosure. *(env.conservation)*

**Consolidation, the** ⏳ — era — EY 1–58; institution-building, two withdrawal crises, the Abolition. *(hist.timeline)*

**Constrained List** ⏳ — policy instrument — Register of materials with a reserve horizon under 150 years; triggers substitution research and consumption ceilings. *(planet.resources)*

**Constrained List extraction levy** ⏳ — tax — Concord levy rising automatically as a material's reserve horizon shortens; the price signal behind recovery rates above 90%. *(econ.money)*

**Contention, the** ⏳ — theatrical form — A staged formal argument in which neither performer may win, each must state the other's position first, and both end by saying what would change their minds. *(cult.arts)*

**Controlled-environment agriculture** ⏳ — production system — 1.1 million facilities producing 24% of Elysian calories on 1/400th of the land field agriculture uses, viable only because energy is cheap. *(agri.production)*

**Convenor** ⏳ — office — Rotating chair of the Executive Board; speaks for a decision, not for a people. *(gov.institutions)*

**Cooperative housing** ⏳ — tenure — 24% of Elysian households; occupancy rights rather than tradeable assets, priced at cost, with contributions indexed to construction costs so nobody profits from the shortage. *(city.housing)*

**Corran Scandal** ⏳ — event — EY 187; a procurement network captured its own auditors for eleven years. Produced the modern Audit Service. *(hist.timeline)*

**Corvet** ⏳ — species — Bird-analogue domesticate kept for eggs. *(agri.production)*

**Council of Regions** ⏳ — institution — Upper chamber of 136; four delegates per Region regardless of population; guardian of decentralization. *(gov.institutions)*

**Court of Review** ⏳ — court — Final appeal on questions of law and harmoniser across the Regions; says what the law means, while the Constitutional Court says what the law may be. *(law.courts)*

## D

**Dependency lock** ⏳ — biosafety requirement — Engineered organisms must require a compound absent in the wild; containment failure is a band 3 offence. *(res.sciences)*

**Design for disassembly** ⏳ — industrial standard — Products must separate into material streams using published tools; permanent bonding requires justification. *(ind.industry)*

**Distributed capital** ⏳ — arrangement — The Concord has no capital city; its institutions sit in five cities on five continents, because a capital is a single point of failure and so is a capital's political culture. *(city.urbanism)*

**District** ⏳ — tier of government — Delivery tier averaging 6.6 million residents; hospitals, schools, transit, utilities, planning. *(gov.regions)*

**Dram** ⏳ — currency — The Concord's currency, symbol d, divided into 100 minims; named from a Thalassic trade weight because it belonged to no region's imperial past. *(econ.money)*

**Durn** ⏳ — species — Large grazing Zoaea domesticate kept for meat and milk. *(agri.production)*

**Duty to intervene** ⏳ — conduct rule — An officer who witnesses excessive force and does not intervene commits an offence of equal gravity to the force itself. *(law.policing)*

**Duty to refuse** ⏳ — Service obligation — Absolute individual duty to refuse a manifestly unlawful order, carrying no penalty for good-faith refusal; the most-taught provision in Service formation. *(mil.service)*

## E

**Ecological Commission** ⏳ — independent office — Monitors the Article 12 constitutional limits; may measure, publish, and compel a response, but not make policy. *(gov.institutions)*

**Ecological Settlement, the** ⏳ — era — EY 40–96; fossil combustion phased out and ecological limits entrenched in the Charter. *(hist.timeline)*

**Education Entitlement Account** ⏳ — right — Twelve Elysian years of fully funded study granted at 16 EY, drawable at any age for any subject, never expiring. Median age at first draw is 24 EY. *(edu.lifelong)*

**Eighth term** ⏳ — teaching requirement — Every teacher spends one term in eight outside the classroom, to stop a profession that teaches about the world from losing contact with it. *(edu.schooling)*

**Elandric** ⏳ — language family — Largest family by native speakers; deep tonal system. *(cult.languages)*

**Elandric Terraces** ⏳ — uplands — Monsoon-fed agricultural uplands of Elandris. *(planet.geography)*

**Elandris** ⏳ — continent — Monsoonal eastern continent; dense coastal urbanism. *(planet.geography)*

**Electoral Commission** ⏳ — independent office — Administers elections and enforces campaign finance. *(gov.institutions)*

**Elysian Concord, the** ⏳ — polity — The planetary civilization of Elysium; name locked as of Phase 2A approval. *(charter.project-charter)*

**Elysians** ⏳ — species — The native sapient Zoaea of Elysium; not related to any Earth lineage. *(hist.timeline)*

**Elysium** — planet — The homeworld of the Concord. Named by the founder. *(charter.project-charter)*

**Emergency of EY 233** ⏳ — event — Emergency powers extended beyond their legal basis; ended by judicial order and the executive's resignation. *(hist.timeline)*

**Equal authenticity** ⏳ — legal principle — Concord legislation is drafted in all 41 registered languages, all equally authoritative; no version is the original. *(cult.languages)*

**Executive Board** ⏳ — institution — Collegial executive of nine; the Concord has no president or prime minister. *(gov.institutions)*

**Extinction debt** ⏳ — measure — 9,000–14,000 species committed to extinction by habitat loss already incurred; published annually and undisputed. *(env.conservation)*

**EY** — notation — "Elysian Year"; years counted from the founding of the Concord. *(charter.canonical-units)*

## F

**Fallow entitlement** ⏳ — labour right — Up to one year away from work every twelve years, job protected and paid at 45%. Defended with no productivity argument. *(econ.markets)*

**Fiscal equalization** ⏳ — mechanism — Transfers based on taxable capacity rather than actual revenue, equalising Regions to 86% of the mean; renegotiated every eight years and fought over every time. *(econ.money)*

**Five Civic Virtues** ⏳ — cultural framework — Candour, stewardship, redundancy, restraint, repair; taught as being in tension with each other. *(cult.foundations)*

**Foundation School** ⏳ — education stage — Ages 6–12 EY; general education with no selection by ability. *(edu.schooling)*

**Fourth Amendment** ⏳ — amendment — Legalised regional withdrawal after the EY 31 crisis; no Region has attempted withdrawal since. *(gov.constitution)*

**Frailty** ⏳ — clinical concept — The organising variable of Elysian medicine, measured routinely from 70 EY; interventions are judged by their effect on it rather than on any single organ system. *(health.practice)*

**Frostmane** ⏳ — species — 900 kg Aurorian browsing herbivore; keystone of boreal nutrient cycling. *(planet.biosphere)*

**Fusion Transition, the** ⏳ — era — EY 88–170; first commercial fusion EY 103, grid transition complete EY 158. *(hist.timeline)*

## G

**Graduated release** ⏳ — corrections practice — Decreasing supervision over the final third of a sentence, typically ending in open conditions with community work. *(law.corrections)*

**Gross Concord Product** ⏳ — measure — Planetary output, d1.03 quadrillion, or d142,000 per capita. *(econ.money)*

## H

**Harm principle** ⏳ — legal doctrine — Conduct is criminal only if it harms or endangers an identifiable interest of another person, the public, or the biosphere. *(law.substantive)*

**Heldan** ⏳ — weekday — First day of the Elysian week, named for Helia. *(hist.calendar)*

**Helia** ⏳ — star — Elysium's G1V primary star; low-variability, 1.12 L☉. *(planet.physical)*

**Helicin** ⏳ — biochemistry — Elysian hereditary molecule; six nucleobases, 4,096 codon meanings, high native error correction. *(planet.biosphere)*

**Holding-period levy** ⏳ — market rule — Transaction levy falling with holding period, from 1.2% under a day to zero above two years; cut turnover by four-fifths. *(econ.markets)*

**Humility clause** ⏳ — constitutional provision — Article 41: prefer purposes over words, and prefer readings that leave room to correct a mistake. *(gov.constitution)*

**Intergenerational account** ⏳ — fiscal instrument — Budget statement of what a budget transfers to and takes from Elysians not yet born; the Office of Future Generations may veto on it alone. *(econ.money)*

## L

**Lake Serapht** ⏳ — lake — Largest freshwater body on Elysium (96,000 km²), Auroria. *(planet.geography)*

**Late-life cognitive decline** ⏳ — condition — Largest cause of disability in the Concord, affecting 21% of Elysians over 110 EY; onset delayed from 96 to 108 EY, with no prevention and no reversal. *(health.practice)*

**Lay assessor** ⏳ — office — One of two residents drawn by lot who sit with a professional judge at first instance with equal votes on verdict and sentence. *(law.courts)*

**Local objection** ⏳ — power — A Commune may compel a published response and public hearing on any decision affecting it, but cannot block. *(gov.regions)*

**Long Emergency, the** ⏳ — era — 78–1 BE; a 78-year cascade of climate, famine, war, plague, and near-catastrophe. *(hist.timeline)*

**Long Maintenance, the** ⏳ — era — EY 300–present; the current, deliberately unheroic era. *(hist.timeline)*

**Long Plateau** ⏳ — scientific era — 140 years without a confirmed advance beyond the standard framework of physics; Elysians disagree whether the wall is physical or institutional. *(res.sciences)*

**Long Reach, the** ⏳ — era — 620–410 BE; Thalassari maritime expansion and the first planetary contact network. *(hist.timeline)*

**Long Summer, the** ⏳ — event — 78 BE; the climate excursion that opened the Long Emergency. *(hist.timeline)*

**Nerith** ⏳ — month — Ninth month. *(hist.calendar)*
